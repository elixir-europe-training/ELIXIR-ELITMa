{%- comment -%} The chapters of a module: the top-level items of its sidebar whose title starts with a two-digit number ("00 ...", "01 ..."). One rule, used by module-chapters.html, module-time.html and module-pager.html. Usage: {% include chapter-items.html nav=some_sidebar %} (nav: a sidebar from site.data.sidebars) Sets chapter_items: an array of those sidebar items (each has .title and .url), in order. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign chapter_items = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign ci_digits = "0123456789" | split: "" -%} {%- for ci_item in include.nav.subitems -%} {%- assign ci_c1 = ci_item.title | slice: 0 -%} {%- assign ci_c2 = ci_item.title | slice: 1 -%} {%- if ci_digits contains ci_c1 and ci_digits contains ci_c2 -%} {%- assign chapter_items = chapter_items | push: ci_item -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
