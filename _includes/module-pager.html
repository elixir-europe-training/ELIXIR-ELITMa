The order comes from the page's sidebar data file: only top-level sidebar items whose title starts with a two-digit number ("00 ...", "11 ...") are part of the sequence, so unnumbered items ("All resources") sit outside it. The count uses those numbers: "00" is shown as "Before you start" and the highest number is the total.
