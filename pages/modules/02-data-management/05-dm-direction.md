In [Chapter 4]({{ site.baseurl }}/04-dm-assess), you assessed how well selected areas of your Node data management strategy are developed and where there is room for improvement. In this chapter, you use those results to decide where your Node wants to go, what role the Node should play and which areas to take forward.

## From assessment to direction

Your assessment shows where you are now, but it does not tell you where to go. A higher maturity level is not automatically the goal: what makes sense depends on your Node context, mandate, needs and available resources. Sometimes you may want to develop something new. In other cases, it may be enough to connect existing activities, make support easier to find or leave an area as it is.

## Decide where the Node should act

An ELIXIR Node does not need to provide everything itself. Expertise, services and responsibilities may already sit with partner institutions, national infrastructures, ELIXIR Platforms or other organisations. Ask where Node involvement would add value. The Node might lead, coordinate, connect or support existing work. Responsibility may also stay with institutions or another organisation, or the area may be outside the current scope of the Node.

For example, partner institutions may already help researchers write data management plans. The Node could help researchers find that support instead of setting up its own. Being clear about the Node role also helps manage expectations: a useful strategy shows what the Node will contribute and what stays with institutions, partners or other organisations.

## Exercise: clarify the Node role

Connect each direction to the governance and responsibilities you mapped in [Chapter 2]({{ site.baseurl }}/02-dm-node-context). For each direction, note what the Node should contribute and who can agree the direction (the decision owner).

In the Level column, note where responsibility for this direction currently sits or should sit:

* **Node**: the Node takes the lead
* **Institutional**: responsibility stays with individual institutions
* **Shared**: the Node and institutions or other partners share responsibility
* **External**: another organisation, such as a national infrastructure or ELIXIR Platform, is responsible
* **Out of scope**: not something the Node needs to address right now

| Strategy area | Desired direction | Level | Node role | Decision owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Add your selected area | Add the desired direction | Node, institutional, shared, external or out of scope | Describe the Node contribution | Note who can agree the direction |

## What your direction and priorities could look like

Imagine that your assessment showed that data stewardship expertise exists across the Node, but roles and capacity are difficult to find.

| Strategy area | Desired direction | Level | Node role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human capacity | Make existing data stewardship expertise easier to find across the Node | Shared | Create a shared overview and connect existing expertise |

This does not require a new data stewardship service. The Node can add value by making better use of expertise that already exists.
