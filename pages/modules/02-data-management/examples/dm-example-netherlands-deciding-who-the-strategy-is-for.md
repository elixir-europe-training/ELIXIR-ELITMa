ELIXIR Netherlands works in a very distributed way. Universities, university medical centres and research organisations already have their own RDM support, tools and expertise. When I started in the new data stewardship liaison role, I began to explore whether the Node needed its own data management strategy.

This raised practical questions. Should the strategy mainly support the core Node team, or the wider Dutch network? Should ELIXIR Netherlands write its own guidance pages, or connect to existing platforms such as RDMkit?

With the Heads of Node, we identified a few areas where ELIXIR Netherlands could help more: data access and governance, metadata standards and catalogues, and training around standards and tools. Deciding where to build something new and where to connect existing work is still the hard part. In a loosely connected network with mostly project funding, that depends on shared priorities across partners.
