In ELIXIR Germany, a lot of research data management work was already happening, but we did not have a clear overview of who was doing what. Different institutes had their own tools, websites and local solutions, and the information was often difficult to find. We wanted to create a shared overview with links, contacts and existing RDM resources in one place, and use it to start conversations between teams.

We started with a small coordination group of people working on data stewardship, training, RDM tools and project coordination. We did not want to involve everyone immediately. First we wanted to make a small start ourselves, see what worked and then share it with the wider network for feedback and additions.

Collecting existing tools, contacts and activities was more difficult than we expected. It was not always clear who maintained local solutions or who to contact, and some institutes had no dedicated data stewards or RDM contacts at all. At some point, we got stuck. A broader coordination perspective, including principal investigators (PIs), would have helped to understand local structures, but this step was not realised in the first phase.

We had no dedicated funding, so collaborating with existing national initiatives such as NFDI became important. Building and maintaining the overview was not easy. Still, the discussions helped start a broader national RDM community conversation that can grow further over time.
