I started as data stewardship liaison for ELIXIR Netherlands in the second quarter of 2026. My role is to connect people who work on data stewardship in the partner organisations. After a first meeting with one of our Heads of Node and a data management expert, I wrote an action plan for my role. One priority comes from the ELIXIR Netherlands strategy: make data stewardship expertise and resources easier to find.

On paper, the plan looked fine. The first action was a stakeholder mapping in the same quarter. Who works on data stewardship in the partner organisations? What do they offer and what do they need? Colleagues commented on the plan in the document and in a meeting with co-leads and representatives. One of our Heads of Node asked for deliverables per quarter and a list of ongoing work. The second version has both.

Carrying it out is harder. The role has no dedicated hours. It competes with my other work and often loses. I talked to some data stewards. The mapping has not happened yet. My actions are not on the Taiga board of ELIXIR Netherlands either. The Heads of Node use that board. Others find it hard to start with. I have not found the time for it yet.

Still, I am happy with what I did. I talked to many people in my new role. I represented ELIXIR Netherlands in data stewardship activities. That takes time when a role is new. Even people I already knew had to get to know me in this role. I am also proud of a website I started with colleagues. It shares practical stories from life sciences and health research in the Netherlands. For example how people set up data stewardship support and what they would do differently. The site is still in development. In time it could make expertise and examples easier to find across the Netherlands.
