In [Chapter 5]({{ site.baseurl }}/05-dm-direction), you decided where your Node wants to go and which priorities to take forward. The next step is a workable route from intention to action. This chapter helps you decide what to do first, who needs to be involved and what you need to make progress.

## Make the priority actionable

A priority gives direction, but it may still be too broad to act on. For example, *make data stewardship expertise easier to find across the Node* is a clear priority. A practical first action could be to create an overview of existing expertise across partner institutions.

Start with an action that is useful and manageable. You can build from there.

## Dependencies and action leads

Some actions can start straight away. Others depend on staff time or on a decision that has not been made yet. Make these dependencies visible in your plan. If something needs to happen first, add it as a step instead of setting a deadline you cannot meet. For example, a shared overview of data stewardship expertise may first need a contact person in each partner institution.

Each action also needs someone who keeps it moving. That person does not have to do everything. The action lead is not always the decision owner from [Chapter 5]({{ site.baseurl }}/05-dm-direction): the decision owner agrees the direction, and the lead follows up between meetings. If no one has the time or mandate to lead an action, the priority may need to wait, become smaller or be taken forward elsewhere.

## What your action plan could look like

Continuing the example from [Chapter 5]({{ site.baseurl }}/05-dm-direction), the first step could look like this:

| Priority | Action | Lead | Resources and dependencies | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Make data stewardship expertise easier to find | Create a first overview of existing roles and expertise | Node coordination | Input from partner institutions and existing role information | First overview within three months |

The next action can follow from what you learn. You do not need to plan every step in advance.

<div class="exercise-box" markdown="1">
## Reflection: is your plan realistic?

Before moving on, check your plan:

* Can the actions start with the people, expertise and resources available?
* Is the necessary funding or decision-making in place?
* Are important dependencies visible?
* Is someone clearly taking each action forward?

Adjust the plan if an action is too large or depends on something that has not yet been agreed.

</div>
