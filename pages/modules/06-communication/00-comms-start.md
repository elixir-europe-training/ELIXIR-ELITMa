This module is for anyone who communicates on behalf of an ELIXIR Node – which, if you coordinate a project, lead a work package or represent your Node anywhere, includes you. You don't need "communications" in your job title, and you don't need any previous training.

It won't turn you into a communications professional. It will help you communicate more deliberately, use what ELIXIR already provides, and know when to ask for help.

## What to expect
Eleven short sections, each around 10–20 minutes – about two and a half hours in total. Each one ends with something you can use straight away.

| Section | Question | What you will take away |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Foundations** | Is communication in your Node working? | A quick audit of your current practice |
| **2. Understanding your audience** | Who needs to hear from you? | A priority matrix and a persona for your top stakeholder |
| **3. Collaboration and sharing** | Who else in the network can help? | A drafted message proposing a joint effort with another Node |
| **4. ELIXIR communications ecosystem** | Which ELIXIR channel for what? | A map of the channels you use – and the ones you're missing |
| **5. Accessibility** | Can everyone use what you publish? | An accessibility check of one of your pages |
| **6. Writing for non-writers** | How do you write so people read? | A tighter opening for a real news item |
| **7. Branding** | Which logo, which look? | A branding sense-check of a recent deck or flyer |
| **8. Graphic design for non-designers** | How do you judge a design? | One slide or flyer, improved |
| **9. Automation and tools** | How do you save time? | One tool set up and working |
| **10. Storytelling and impact** | How do you show what changed? | Your two-minute impact pitch |
| **11. Strategy canvas** | How does it all fit together? | The first row of your Node's communications strategy |

## How the pages work
Every section uses the same few building blocks, so you always know what you're looking at:

* **Your turn** – exercises are in orange boxes. They take a few minutes and work best with a real project in front of you.
* **Checklists** – tick them as you go. Your ticks are remembered in your browser, so you can come back later.
* **Real-world examples** – dark blue cards link to how an ELIXIR Node actually did it. They're short, and often the most useful part.
* **Templates** – download buttons give you editable PowerPoint and Word files to reuse.
* **Dive deeper** – the table at the end of each page lists further resources. Some are on the ELIXIR intranet and need your consortium login.

## Bring a real project
The exercises are much more useful applied to something real than done in the abstract. Before you start, pick **one current or upcoming output from your Node** – a new service, an event, a project result, a training course – and keep it in mind as you go. By the end, you'll have worked out who it's for, how to say it, where to share it and how to tell whether it worked.

<div class="exercise-box" markdown="1">
### Quick check before you start
Take a minute to think about your starting point.

* Which output or project will you use as your example?
* Who communicates for your Node today – and do you know who does it in other Nodes?
* Do you already have a communications plan or strategy, even an informal one?
* What's the one communication problem you'd most like to solve?
